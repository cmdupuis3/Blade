1. **G35: reuse the partition, not the existing partial reduction.** `src/CodeGenLoopNest.fs:1605-1609,2132-2140,2195-2200` answers the seed question: ChunkSerial starts from its first contributed value; ChunkSimd starts from 0/1; the caller's seed is already in the outer accumulator and enters the combine ONCE. Preserve every inner fold's own seed and order. Newly emitted `W/pair-repro.cpp:226-233,296-301` and `W/comoment-repro.cpp:220-241,295-300` confirm the benchmark's nested serial chains. Qualify byte identity with the stated contract-off regime; identical source operation trees alone do not establish fast-contraction identity.

3. **G35: panic refusal is necessary; the work threshold is only a hypothesis.** `src/cpp/blade_runtime.hpp:361-390` uses `panicking.exchange(1)`: the first arriving worker prints and exits, others spin. 6. **The G=1 first-failure proof holds only for f, with total fold kernels and preserved locations.** A sorted symmetric tuple is its lexicographically first permutation. Preserve original SrcLoc when relocating a potentially failing G=1 f; equal cells do not automatically preserve diagnostic attribution/backtraces.

## disagreements I could not settle from source

- **Performance and gates:** no source proof selects 2^18, G=4/B=64, or establishes scatter/G35/C profitability. Runner must time matched emitted walks and joined spellings, interleaved at non-power-of-two extents with medians/CI. Rank-2 parity is plausible, not demonstrated; the universal 50% acceptance rule needs replacing (items 10-13).
- **NaN contract:** C explicitly waives sNaN differences as print-invisible, while the preamble demands strict chains/byte identity. The arithmetic counterexample is settled; whether the language intentionally excludes payload/signalling state from the promised identity needs a third-reviewer policy ruling. Until then, use true first-element initialization or decline the unproved domain (item 14).
- **LLVM contraction fencing:** I verified the scalar emitter and emitted join flags, not a new scatter emitter. Whether removing contract only from the term's last fmul prevents all relevant backend contractions, and whether arithmetic.fence plus custom vector types is the desired infrastructure, needs a focused LLVM implementation/assembly test. A final-value C++ barrier is already disproved for general f (item 8).
- **Post-pass integration:** no shareSiblingFoldsModule exists to execute. Source shows a viable let-bound encoding, but capture hygiene, derived callable tables, mixed-policy joins and B/E interactions need implementation-level differential/emission evidence. The reproduced BL7004 fixture alone neither proves nor blocks those paths (items 15-20).
