1. **blocker ? Inlining moves reads across writes before the interval analysis even starts.** `src/Optimize.fs:2653-2671,2964,3088-3090`. `buildUnits` inserts a call's entire spine BEFORE its containing statement. A group consisting of two folds inside that call ends before the original statement, so `crossesWrite` never sees an earlier writing subexpression left in that statement. Example: `stats(x)` returns `reduce(x,(+)) + prodsum(x,x)` through two lets; `bump(x)` writes `x(0)=50.0` through a mut parameter and returns 0.0; evaluate `let answer = bump(x) + stats(x)` on `[1.0,2.0,3.0]`. Both are named calls, so `quickHasFold` enters; bump refuses inlining, stats qualifies, its two folds join, and its whole computation moves before bump. In the interpreter's left-to-right evaluation this changes 2568.0 to 20.0. The writing original unit is outside the tentative group's interval. **Fix:** preserve evaluation-prefix ordering when extracting calls; minimally refuse extraction from any containing statement that may write, including transitive/deferred effects. Test a writing call before a multi-fold callee in ONE RHS, not merely between two let-bound folds. A group wholly inside an extracted callee should not justify moving that callee across its caller's effects.

2. **blocker ? Shared-fold structural projections are remapped as flat indices.** `src/Optimize.fs:2888-2890,2925-2932,3120-3122`. The flat destructuring in corpus 005 misses this. **Fix:** preserve/reconstruct the original tuple shape from the new leg variables, or translate structural paths using the original leaf ranges. Alternatively decline shared trees with any non-flat projection until that translation exists. Add direct indexing and nested/partial destructuring regressions.

Audit notes for the remaining attack points:

- Totality (`Optimize.fs:2443-2558,2794-2808,2913-2915`) checks fold/map kernels, seeds, and resolved call bodies recursively, rejecting recursive/unknown callees, writes, output, subscripts/guards, float-to-int casts, lgamma/digamma. Typed floating `/` is discharged, so the fusedchain squash can qualify. It also permits proven-safe literal integer divisors/moduli and floating powers; the prose ?integer / % decline? is broader than the implementation. These safe cases are not themselves a defect. Static equal operand extents are checked; streamed storage is the missing proof in finding 3.
- Ordinary interval barriers include both endpoint statements, known/unknown writing calls, direct assignments and lowered let-rec loops. Element writes through aliases and transitive named writing calls are barriers regardless of which array they write. A non-streamed provider read already materialized before the folds is not a mutable-storage counterexample. Mut parameters/views are not categorically refused; without an intervening write, reading them is safe. The actual holes are extraction before analysis and incomplete deferred/storage provenance.
- Thread and policy gates (`Optimize.fs:2384-2386,2770-2773,2986-2996`) use Toolchain's exact per-call predicates. `FP_REASSOC=0/off` and OMP thread-count `1` semantics survive the move. Per-leg callables are retained; no request/licence union is introduced.
- Dedupe (`Optimize.fs:2753-2768,3000-3017`) alpha-renames parameters but retains referenced capture IDs and callee IDs; two kernels reading DIFFERENT captures do not become equal merely because their scalar operator shape agrees. Float literals use bit keys, including seeds. Existing multi-leg trees are not internally deduplicated; identical standalone legs are. Joins are always let-bound; the new flat tuple itself is correct (`3103-3104`), distinct from the old structural-projection bug.
- Inliner exclusions include repro/static/arity/generic/cuda/mpi/omp, captures, recursive self references, non-atom/function-valued args and non-scalar results. `freshenCallable` preserves operation SrcLoc fields and referenced callable attributes; the enclosing callee's policy attributes disappear with the call, which is why these exclusions matter. - Seeds (`Optimize.fs:2300-2310,2800-2806`) match arbitration Q1, including literal n>=1 for unseeded legs. `IRLitFloat32` renders `0.0f` (`CodeGenExprSupport.fs:526-532`), so negation is `-(0.0f)`.
