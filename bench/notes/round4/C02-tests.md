## Read-only LLVM allocation diagnosis

At root's request, the exact test source was lowered and passed to `EmitLlvm.tryEmitProgramNamed` from FSI against the just-built `Blade.dll`; no generated program was executed. The source was copied from `partialRowsSrc` in `OptimizeTests.fs`, with `BLADE_FP_REPRO=1`, `BLADE_FP_CONTRACT=off`, empty `BLADE_PRINT`, `BLADE_BLAS=off`, and fusion toggled `on`/`0`. Raw IR and emitted LLVM artifacts are under [`llvm-allocation-diagnostic`](../../../../c-terminals/bench/.build/C02-runner/llvm-allocation-diagnostic), with exact commands/environment in its provenance files.

The failing assertion counts `call ptr @blade_alloc_cells(` in the whole LLVM string and requires ON=2 and OFF=3. Direct counts from the same binary are ON=2 and OFF=2. Each output contains one function definition, `@main`; there are no outlined callable definitions containing hidden extra allocation sites. In both routes the calls are in `main`: `%r2 = call ptr @blade_alloc_cells(i64 6, i64 8)` allocates the required M input, immediately filled with its six literal values, and `%r9 = call ptr @blade_alloc_cells(i64 2, i64 8)` allocates the required two-row result. Neither route allocates or fills a separate six-cell mapped temporary in LLVM.

The OFF IR still has `let v12 = ...` for the rank-2 map and then applies the row fold to `v12`; its `[partial-row-fold]` decision says declined because the gate is disabled. The LLVM backend emits the producer's scalar map arithmetic directly inside the fold's row/cell loops and reads M at `row*3+column`, so the private mapped R×C value has no materialized pool even with this optimization disabled. The ON IR decision applies and binds the new `__partial_rows_24` row callable with a concrete C row parameter and no captures; its `IRReduceCompute` carries literal seed `7.0`. The original map/fold callables (`__lambda_17`, `__lambda_20`) and row callable (`__lambda_21`) have no captures. The output's array and print path retain M and rows. Thus the assertion failure is the OFF expectation of a third LLVM pool; this diagnostic does not show a production storage regression.

The approved 035 demanded-output control was also lowered/emitted ON and OFF without execution. Both LLVM outputs have one `@main` definition and three allocation calls: 6×8 bytes for M, 6×8 bytes for named P, and 2×8 bytes for rows. Both retain print strings for `M`, `P`, and `rows`; IR binding `sample` reads `P` at `(1,2)`. The optimizer explicitly declines `rows` with “top-level named/printed producers retain their storage.” This confirms a three-pool demanded negative control. Its raw IR, `.ll`, logs, and provenance are alongside the diagnostic in `llvm-allocation-diagnostic/demanded-035`.
