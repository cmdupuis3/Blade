## Finding in brief

**The checked mathematics held up. The next hardening work is chiefly at the boundaries of the claims.** All 62 modules rebuilt and passed independent checking. All 1,960 named results in the core tower are closed under the global context. No invalid theorem, admission, hidden added axiom, or disabled kernel safety check was found. The latest Prouhet-shift proofs also passed scrutiny.

The audit found **20 distinct actionable items**, with overlap between the tranche reports deduplicated below. They include one concrete helper-function defect, false explanatory claims, insufficient coverage for natural examples, and gaps between conditional numerical theorems and a proposed floating-point implementation. These do **not** collectively mean the proof system is unsound, and none establishes a shipped Blade compiler miscompile.

The highest-value results are:

- A normal two-pass floating-point seed can make the error theorem's initialization premise impossible: the exact first centered sum is 0 while the computed value is 2. This occurs without overflow or underflow.
- Approximate symmetry is not preserved through the relaxed calculus's arbitrary pipeline functions. A fully typed parity example goes from equivalent values 3 and 1 to inequivalent values 1 and 0 after division by 2.
- Jacobian symmetry swaps derivative channels as well as inputs. It does not justify ordinary triangular storage for each scalar partial. The kernel `x*y` supplies a checked counterexample.
- One six-component access recipe reverses noncommuting permutations. Following the prose returns 0 where the theorem's recipe correctly returns 1.
- `BladeLayout.best_cost` reports 99 for a problem whose actual minimum is 100. The separately proved canonical scheduler remains correct.
- The general recurrence quotient theorem cannot cover the file's own natural reversed-prefix example: its arbitrary-reader premise is too strong.
- Repository CI does not run the proofs, and the theorem-count check succeeds even when a proof is missing from its manifest.

## Recommended hardening order

1. **Correct the concrete definitions and recipes.** Replace the 99 sentinel, correct the permutation order, expose a checked compiler entry point, and keep their counterexamples as permanent regression examples. Add agreement between `best_cost` and the proved canonical scheduler.
2. **Close the float admission chain before relying on the proposed guarantee.** Prove seed accuracy using absolute/mixed errors, restrict stability to moments actually read, certify an upper bound on true rho, tie N to particle count, and carry positive-denominator/radicand and finite-operation obligations. A conditional propagation bound cannot supply its own initial/domain hypotheses.
3. **Strengthen composition and storage contracts.** Require R-congruence for post-pipes; prove a quotient theorem over full typed expressions. Give Jacobian canonical reads an explicit channel action. Replace arbitrary-reader recurrence invariance with a reachable invariant-reader condition or simultaneous equivariance, and derive the full `sym_step` agreement theorem.
4. **Make the map as precise as the mathematics.** Fix the biconditional, rank-zero exception, host-type necessity, geometric-growth necessity, fixed-summary refusal, exact counts, and characteristic assumptions. Preserve good existing scope ledgers instead of converting conditional results into blanket claims.
5. **Automate assurance.** Add all three proof manifests to CI, independent checks, per-result assumption allowlists, count/manifest coverage failures, and durable adversarial examples. Connect model oracles to actual Blade corpus/AST cases where implementation correspondence is claimed.

## Important boundaries that were checked and should remain explicit

The audit did not promote every missing generalization to a defect. Existing boundaries include rank-2 safety over unbounded naturals, oracle-model rather than wall-clock optimality, finite C4/D4 and low-rank table checks, contiguous-block deduction precision, exact ring versus IEEE arithmetic, and the absence of a verified F# compiler bridge. The detailed reports record these separately from findings.

In particular:

- Zero ranks, empty extents, zero cardinalities, natural subtraction, malformed tuples, diagonal ties, noninjective data, and characteristic assumptions were challenged across the enumeration/storage proofs.
- Optimality claims were checked against their oracle freedom and storage-model premises; arbitrary encodings and shared arithmetic are outside those models.
- Recurrence budgets, guards, discarded observations, finite horizons, special parameters, small extents, dual-number identities, and coefficient zero tests were checked against their quantifiers.
- Numeric probes addressed zero moments, signed cancellation, constant ensembles, division/square-root domains, true versus approximate invariants, and feedback error correlation.
- The historical `par`/`parfix` distinction in `BladeDeduceExact` was investigated and rejected as a finding: the header deliberately distinguishes the old rule from the corrected compiler rule.
- The documented signed-Int64 wrapping gap remains a known implementation boundary. This audit did not relabel the modular theorem as a proof of undefined-overflow C++ behavior.
